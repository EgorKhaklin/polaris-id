Issuer Federation

Explicit-only trust attestations. An attesting agency vouches that an attested agency may verify in a context, until valid_until or explicit revocation. The _federation_trust_holds() check gates SUCCESS outcomes on verifications crossing agency boundaries. No transitive trust: if A vouches for B and B vouches for C, A does NOT implicitly vouch for C. Same-agency verifications are implicit.

How federation gating works

  1. Same-agency verification: when the verifying agency is the same as the issuing agency, no attestation lookup is needed. Trust is implicit at issuance.
  2. Cross-agency verification: the verifications_new handler calls _federation_trust_holds(verifier_agency_id, token_id, context_id) before flipping the outcome to SUCCESS. The check joins AgencyTrustAttestation filtered to the context and to today's date, with revocation_date IS NULL.
  3. Explicit-only: there is no recursive walk. A → B and B → C does NOT imply A → C. If you want A → C, write that attestation.
  4. Audit-of-record: AgencyTrustAttestation is an audit-of-record instance. Rows are append-only at the data-content level (an immutability trigger blocks mutation of any field except revocation_date and revocation_reason); revocation is recorded as a state transition, not a delete.

Mechanism design: docs/design/federation.md. Concurrency: per-attesting-agency lock is the 5th entry in docs/design/concurrency.md.
