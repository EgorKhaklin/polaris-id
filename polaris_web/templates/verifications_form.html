The rule

A zero-knowledge verification stores no credential, and a full one always names its credential; the database refuses any other combination.

A success is recorded only when it could have happened: the credential is active and within its validity, permitted in the context, and its issuer is trusted by the verifier for that context. Anything else is recorded as the outcome it had.

The disclosure rule is the chk_disclosure_token_consistency CHECK.
