Out-of-band channels

Each PENDING request requires three independent channels verified before an APPROVED decision is permitted:

  • B: Biometric: holder presents at the requesting agency for live biometric capture.
  • S: Sworn statement: holder signs an affidavit; only its hash is stored (a hash-only commitment, never the plaintext).
  • W: Witness agency: an independent agency co-signs the recovery.

These channels are designed to have independent failure modes. Compromising one is not enough. See docs/design/recovery-ceremony.md for the adversary walk.
