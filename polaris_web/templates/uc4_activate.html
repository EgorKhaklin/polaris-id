The rule

A person holds one active credential at a time. The lost credential ends first, its revocation is published, and only then does the reserve become active, naming the lost one as its predecessor; a failure at any step changes nothing.

The reserve belongs to the same holder, is still a reserve when the change is made, and has not passed its expiry.

Enforced in the database by uc4_activate_reserve.
