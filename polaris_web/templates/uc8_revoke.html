The rule

An authority may revoke only so much of its own population in a rolling window: by default 5% of its outstanding credentials in 30 days. Past that bound a second authority must co-sign: an authority other than the actor, authorized both to issue and to verify under the credential's algorithm.

An active, reserve or dormant credential can be revoked; a revoked, lost or expired one cannot.
