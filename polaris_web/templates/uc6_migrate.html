Use Case 6

Algorithm Migration

Multi-signature transitional state. Adds a new TokenSignature row under a new algorithm, optionally deprecating the old. The old signature continues to verify until its deprecation_date passes: this is the migration window. The TokenSignature row is the audit of record for migrations (no separate event table). Cryptographic diversity, bounded revocation and federation together keep any one issuer from concentrating trust.

What this UC does NOT do:

  • Does not generate a real cryptographic signature, placeholder bytes are inserted (the development signing path).
  • Does not auto-deprecate based on CryptographicAlgorithm.deprecation_date: operator policy is the only path.
  • Does not remove the old TokenSignature row, the append-only invariant forbids DELETE.
