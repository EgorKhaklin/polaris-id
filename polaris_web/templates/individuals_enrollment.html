Enrollment vocabulary

NOT_ENROLLED
Default state for every new Individual. The absence of enrollment, materialized but not weaponized.
PENDING_ENROLLMENT
Enrollment process initiated; biometrics or documentation in progress.
ENROLLED
Holds at least one non-terminal IdentityToken (RESERVE or ACTIVE). Recorded as a policy event, not auto-derived from token state.
EXEMPT
Civic policy recognition of non-token participation (biometric incompatibility, religious exemption, conscientious objection). The accepted path without a token.
LAPSED
Was ENROLLED, now isn't, by policy event. Distinct from NOT_ENROLLED (never enrolled).

Per-individual enrollment history is recorded in the append-only EnrollmentStatusEvent table. See docs/design/tiered-enrollment.md for the asymmetric-design rationale.
