What follows

  1. The request opens as pending, and a 48-hour cool-down starts.
  2. Three out-of-band channels are recorded by someone other than the requester: the biometric check, a sworn statement, and a co-sign from a second authority.
  3. A different administrator decides it. Approval ends every credential the person still holds, publishing each revocation, and issues a new active one.

A person who holds an active credential is not recovered: losing it is a reserve activation. One recovery is pending for a person at a time.
