\section{The metacognitive map}
\label{app:layers}

The ladder below is the paper's answer to the request for an explicit map of Polaris as layers of
knowledge and scepticism. Each rung states what the layer knows and what it refuses to take on faith
from the rung below. The layers are not exactly the rings of Figure~\ref{fig:core-to-shell}, because
some rings hold two kinds of knowledge; the rungs follow the implementation. Version 3 adds a rung at
the top, for the knowledge the tree cannot check about itself; Figure~\ref{fig:layer-ladder} draws the
ladder after the table.
