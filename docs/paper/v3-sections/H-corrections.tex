keep the voice of the record; the map in the body does not depend on them.

\subsection*{Since Version 2: the instruments were asked whether they would notice}

The question changed from \emph{does it pass} to \emph{would it notice}, and the answers below are
the record. Most of these rows are about the tests rather than the system, which is the point: a
guarantee is believed because a check says so, and the checks are written by the same hand as the
code.

\subsection*{The walls, asked whether they would notice}

Version 2 described the checks and stopped there. Between \code{v9.398} and \code{v9.437} the
repository asked the layer the question it asks everything else, not \emph{does it pass} but
\emph{would it notice}, and the answer was often no. Commenting out the lines carrying a check's
search strings, which is exactly what a temporarily disabled line looks like, left 71 of 75 checks
green, because the helper every check reads through handed it the comments along with the code.
Dropping each of the 46 CHECK constraints found every one load-bearing, which was the good news;
dropping each of the 37 triggers found 14 that nothing noticed, twelve of them the append-only guards
that C1 \emph{is}; dropping each of the 17 unique indexes found twelve uncovered, and stopped on the
second, because with \code{uq\_one\_active\_per\_person} absent the suite that exists to prove C3 had
inserted and committed a second active token for one person and the index could not be rebuilt.
Deleting each of the 59 refusals the sixteen stored procedures make found 27 that no test would miss,
concentrated exactly where a trigger cannot see: the four-eyes rule, the cool-down, the three channels
and the third-person witness of the recovery ceremony, the preconditions on an irreversible erasure.
Every one of these is now covered. The drill selected procedures only, and three of the use cases,
issuance, reserve activation and device binding, are functions, because they return the id they
create; their eleven refusals sat outside it until 23 September 2026. Among the ones no test
noticed were the check that an agency is authorized for the algorithm it issues under and the check that a reserve token belongs
to the same person as the credential it replaces. Nine are covered now. One of them, the reserve check the routine repeats under its lock, can
fire only if another transaction moves the reserve while the routine waits, so its test holds the
lock itself, waits until the routine is seen blocked on it, revokes the reserve and lets go. The
two that remain are the copies of its two status checks made before the lock, which on one connection
refuse exactly when their twins do. Every one of these
measurements is now a drill that runs on
every push with a negative control, so that a result of zero survivors is distinguishable from a
harness that ran nothing. Twelve such drills exist at \code{1.0.0-rc.7}: over the checks, the
