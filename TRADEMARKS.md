The [Apache License](LICENSE) covers Polaris's code, documents and image files. It grants no
right to use the names and marks those files carry (section 6 of the license). This page says
what that means in practice.

| Mark | Files |
|---|---|
| The Polaris and Polaris ID names, as the names of this project and its packages | throughout |
| The Polaris logo and star mark | `site/polaris_logo_clean.png`, `site/favicon.svg`, `polaris_web/static/favicon.svg` |
| The owl emblem | `docs/assets/seal.svg` |
| The Khaklin Technologies name and lockup, with the Polaris logo | `docs/assets/hero.svg` |

[REUSE.toml](REUSE.toml) declares these files. They are under Apache 2.0 like the rest of the
tree: the license covers the files, not the marks.

## What you can do without asking

- Say that your work is based on, forked from, compatible with or built with Polaris.
- Redistribute Polaris unmodified under its own name, logo included.
- Use the name and logo to write about or link to the project.

## What needs your own name

A fork, a modified build or a product built on Polaris carries its own name and logo, not the
Polaris name or logo, the owl, or the Khaklin Technologies name, and does not suggest that the
project or its maintainer endorses it.

## A mark that is not the project's

The OpenID Certified mark (`openid-certified-mark-on-white.png`, in `docs/assets/` and `site/`)
belongs to the OpenID Foundation and is used under its certification terms, beside the scoped
statement of what is certified: polaris-oid4vp 1.0.0rc7 is OpenID Certified to the
OpenID4VP 1.0 + HAIP 1.0 Verifier profile. Neither the project's license nor this page grants any
right to the mark, and a fork or a later release is not certified.
